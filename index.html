FAQ

Common questions

Will I be charged before I'm approved?

No. Applying is free and we don't ask for payment details. Only approved applicants receive a payment link, sent by us by email. If we decline your application, you pay nothing.

How much does it cost?

Standard membership is $1,199 for 12 months. There is also a two-month trial for a one-time $99. Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT and other applicable taxes.

I'm already a founding member. What do I pay?

Founding members of the community pay $699 for 12 months, through a private link we email to you. The founding rate is not available to new applicants. Founding members keep the $699 rate on renewal while their membership stays continuously active. If you leave and come back later, the then-current Standard price applies.

How does the two-month trial work?

You pay $99 once and get two months of full access. The trial fee is non-refundable and is not deducted from the annual price. After two months your access ends and you are not charged again. To continue, you purchase the Standard annual membership at $1,199.

Does my membership renew automatically?

No. An annual membership is a single payment for 12 months of access, and you are never charged again automatically. About two weeks before it ends we email you with a link to renew. If you don't renew, your access simply ends and we remove you from the Discord server.

Can I get a refund?

Annual memberships are refundable if you ask within 30 days of payment. After 30 days they are not refundable. The trial fee is non-refundable. Details are in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Do you give investment advice or trading signals?

No. Hot Tottie is a community where members discuss markets and analyse investments for informational and educational purposes only. We don't give personalised advice, sell signals, manage money, or guarantee results. Always do your own research.

Are the conversations private?

Yes. Everything shared in the Club is confidential to members, and members agree not to record, screenshot or share it outside the Club. Our administrators and moderators can see what is posted.

Who can apply, and from where?

Experienced long/short traders aged 18 or over, from any country. Admission is selective and every application is reviewed by a person. Taxes may apply depending on where you live.

Is the Long/Short Terminal included?

No. It's a separate software product sold separately. Members get a discount.

What payment methods do you accept?

The methods available at Whop's checkout (major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay), processed securely by Whop. We never see your card details.

How do I get access after I pay?

We invite you to the private Discord server by hand, normally within one business day of your payment, and within 24 hours even on non-business days. Make sure we have your Discord username.

How do I contact you?

Email info@hot-tottie.org. We reply within 2 business days.
